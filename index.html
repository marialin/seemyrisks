How to use it in three minutes

1

Group your holdings

Add each holding with its dollar value and the type closest to it. Apple and Microsoft are "Tech / Nasdaq-100", Nvidia or SanDisk "Chips / AI hardware", an S&P 500 fund "US broad index".

2

Be honest about borrowing

Margin loans, leveraged ETFs and loans against your shares all count. 2x means you hold $2 of stock for every $1 of your own money.

3

Compare, don't predict

The next crash will be different. Use the gap between you and SPY or QQQ to see whether you are taking more risk than you think.
